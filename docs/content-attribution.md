## Changes

Thirty source examples were selected and adapted into 90 exercises across three
answer modes. Changes include replacing a target form with a gap, adding explicit
grammatical cues and four distractors, writing bilingual explanations, translating
into Ukrainian, and normalizing punctuation and capitalization. The short source
phrase about a can of beer is retained as a phrase rather than expanded into an
invented source sentence. Source names, author-history links, the license and an
adaptation notice are displayed with every adapted exercise.

These are adaptations of teaching examples, not copies of an existing interactive
question bank. IDs and context families are stable and use the `wikibooks-` prefix.
