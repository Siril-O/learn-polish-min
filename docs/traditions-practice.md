# Traditions practice

The culture pool includes original multiple-choice questions about Polish customs.
Each new question has a stable ID and family ID, five choices, Polish and Ukrainian
explanations, and a source URL. Cultural references support the answers; preparation
sites and reported interviews inform topic selection, not an official question bank.

## Interview relevance

- [Polish embassy: interview includes Polish customs and traditions](https://www.gov.pl/web/lotwa/karta-polaka-informacje-ogolne).
- [Published account of a 2019 interview concerning Easter and Christmas Eve](https://wolnoscodreligii.pl/jak-pan-obchodzi-wielkanoc-i-wigilie-rozmowy-konsula-na-karte-polaka-list-do-fundacji/).
  This is a third-party anecdote, not an independently verified transcript or evidence
  that every interview asks these questions.
- [Preparation topics for the consular interview](https://mavial.pl/karta-polaka/rozmowa-z-konsulem.html).
  This is an unofficial preparation resource, not a guaranteed examination syllabus.

Questions are independently worded, not copied from commercial question banks.
Religious and regional customs are described as traditions, not universal practices
of every Polish family. Personal interview answers should reflect the learner's real
experience rather than a fabricated family story.
